faq:
  title: "Questions"
  items:
    - q: "How does HourWarden know which customer I work for?"
      a: "It uses the same information your taskbar shows: the name of the program and the title of its window, such as 'Offer Contoso.docx - Word'. It compares that with what you entered for your customers (their names, folders and mail domains). Plugins you choose to install can add a little more, such as the folder of the open document or the domain of the open mail; each plugin says what it reads before you install it. Everything is worked out on your own computer."
    - q: "What does HourWarden not do?"
      a: "It does not record what you type (it only notices whether you are at the computer, so time away does not count), take screenshots, or read the text of your documents or mails; the Outlook plugin reads only a mail's subject and addresses. It never uses your camera or microphone; the Teams plugin only checks whether Teams is using the microphone, to know a call is on. There is no tracking and no analytics: apart from sync, the app only asks hourwarden.com whether there is a new version and loads the plugin store from GitHub. You can pause it at any time, and change or delete any entry."
    - q: "Does my data leave my computer?"
      a: "No, not unless you turn on sync. The activity log, with everything you had open, never leaves your computer. Sync carries your customers, tasks and hours between your own devices, through our server in the EU or your own; each hour entry includes its task, your notes, the program and a short description of what it started with, such as a document name. End-to-end encryption is on by default, so the server cannot read any of it."
    - q: "Can I use it without customers, to track my own work?"
      a: "Yes. Choose 'I track my own work' in the setup guide: all time goes to your own company, and HourWarden shows what you did per program, website and kind of work."
    - q: "Which systems does it run on?"
      a: "Windows 11 (installer or portable zip), Linux with KDE Plasma (.deb and .rpm) and Android. The app and the help are in English and Dutch."
    - q: "What does it cost?"
      a: "The app is free and works without an account. Sync needs an account; your account page shows its plan and limits."
    - q: "How do I keep up with new versions?"
      a: "The Windows app offers updates itself; on Linux, Android and the portable version a notice says when a new version is out. The release notes are on this site, also as an RSS feed."
