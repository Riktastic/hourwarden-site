- **Suggestions on Today**: customers HourWarden saw you work for during unknown time (by their domains, tenant or DevOps organisation), made with one click; plugins for the programs and sites you use.
- **Track your own work**: a choice at the start of the setup guide for people who do not book hours per customer. All time goes to your company and Today puts what you did first.
- **Fix unknown time in one go**: the Daily review groups unknown time by what was in front, to assign (and remember) per group. Tick several entries to change or delete them together, drag the start or end of an entry on the timeline, and undo a change for a minute.
- **Questions at the end of the day**, if you like: the top bar does not ask during work; Today shows what is unknown.
- **Why this customer?**: the top bar explains its choice, and *Wrong customer* moves the whole stretch to the one you pick.
- **Budget per week**: hours per week for a customer on its page; Today shows each budget this week (amber from 80%, red when over) and Reports per week and customer.
- **Quieter settings**: fine-tuning you never changed sits behind *More settings*.
- **Phone**: a quick settings tile starts and stops the timer without opening the app, and *Add the meeting I just had* offers appointments from the phone's calendar that ended in the last three hours.
