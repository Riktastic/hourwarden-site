- **Settings in tabs**: General, Tracking, Focus, Breaks and Backups instead of one long page. Searching still looks through all of them; the last tab is remembered.
- **Quotes that cheer you on**: a quote of the day on the Today screen and one on every break screen, from people such as Seneca, Marcus Aurelius, Spinoza, Franklin and Van Gogh, each with where it comes from. Turn them off with *Show quotes* under Breaks.
- **Your own quotes**: add them under Breaks, one per line (*Keep going.*); they show up among the others.
- Error messages of the new plugins are now in Dutch too.
