- **End-to-end encryption on by default**: the first device of an account turns it on after its first sync and shows the key until you confirm you stored it. Accounts whose other devices sync without it are left as they are.
- **Plans on the server**: the administrator sets up tiers (customers, storage, how long hours stay on the server, devices) and gives accounts one, with or without an end date. With Stripe set up, users can pick another plan on their account page and manage it in Stripe's billing page; card and bank details never reach the server. When a limit is reached, new data stays on the device and the app says why.
- **Messages to the administrator**: *Questions or problems?* on the account page. The administrator answers in the admin panel; a mail (if set up) only says there is a message.
- **Support page per account** in the admin panel: why sync was last refused, plan and usage, devices, payments, messages, and a reset for a lost encryption key.
- **Admin panel only from your address**: `hourwarden-server admin allow me` over SSH.
- **Account page from the app**: *Account page* under Sync opens the server's page for your password, plan and messages.
- **@ lists your customers**: typing @ in a task's quick-add field shows the matching customers to pick.
- **Month tab** in Time, per day or per week, with CSV and Excel export like the week. Each Time tab says what it is for; *Hours* is now *Totals*.
- **A calmer Today**: what is tracked now comes first, and unknown time is shown once.
