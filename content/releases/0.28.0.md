- **Ask before switching customer**: an option under Tracking. When the window in front belongs to another customer, the top bar asks *Switch to Fabrikam?*; until you answer, time stays with the customer you were working for. Yes moves the time since then; no keeps it.
- **Missing hours**: with your work days, work hours and break set (Settings → Tracking → Timesheet) and *Check for missing hours* on, Today shows what is missing this week and Reports the difference per day. The work hours moved there from Slack.
- **Billable or internal**: mark a customer or project as internal (administration, training, sales); your own company always is. Reports show the billable and non-billable percentages per week, with the change against the week before, and per customer, with export; Today shows *Billable this week*.
- **SharePoint and OneDrive sites as customer folders**: paste a site's address on the customer page; its pages in the browser and documents opened from it in Office count for that customer.
- **The customer by tenant, workspace or project**: the Azure, Fabric and Azure DevOps plugins pass on where you are (tenant, subscription, resource group, workspace, organisation, project). The question about unknown time can remember the answer for that resource group, project or workspace, and once a tenant or organisation belongs to a customer, what is seen on the same page is linked to that customer by itself.
- **Plugins' log of unknown pages is off by default**; turn on *Note pages it does not know* to report one.
- **Activity log for everyone**: every program and site counts as a kind of work (email, meetings, chat, documents, development, research…), so the time per kind also shows without plugins or customers. The list shows things, sites (pages of one site together) or programs. Reports has *What the time went to*: per kind of work, program or site, next to the period before, to CSV or Excel. Optionally, entries without notes get the list of what you did. IDs can get names; IDs in a customer's mail domains get the customer's name.
- **Activity log privacy**: a list of programs, sites and title words never to record, and *Pause the activity log* in the tray.
- **Plugins note pages they do not know**: the Fabric, Azure and Azure DevOps plugins write the shape of such an address (without IDs or values) to their log, to report.
- **Activity log**: HourWarden records what you work on, each document, page, mail, server or meeting and how long. Every entry in Review shows what it consisted of, and *Add to notes* puts that list in its notes; Today shows what you did today. It stays on this computer and is removed after 90 days.
- **Azure DevOps plugin**: the work item, board, pull request, repository, pipeline run, release, test plan or wiki page you have open, with organisation and project; a work item is offered as the task. Counted as planning, code review, development, CI/CD, testing or documentation.
- **Azure plugin**: the Azure resource you have open, its resource group and what you look at (configuration, logs, access control…), and the Microsoft admin centres. Counted as infrastructure, data, security and identity, monitoring, integration, AI, cost and governance, or administration.
- **Microsoft Fabric and Power BI plugin**: recognises the report, semantic model, notebook, lakehouse, warehouse or pipeline you have open in the browser, and when you edit a semantic model; also Power BI Desktop, Tabular Editor, DAX Studio and SSMS on a workspace's XMLA endpoint. The activity log keeps the tenant and workspace from the web-addresses plugin, and each Fabric item counts as a kind of work (reporting, data modelling, data engineering, data integration, data warehousing, real-time analytics, data science, administration); entries and Today show the time per kind.
- **Slack you choose**: a switch per kind of slack (social media, video, news, shopping, games). For a social media or account manager, social media is work: turn it off and it is never counted or reminded about. Your own slack sites can have a kind (*Funda: shopping*), and 27 more sites are recognised, among them LinkedIn, Zalando, de Volkskrant and NPO Start. Sites are now recognised by their web address (with the web-addresses plugin) instead of only the tab title, so Reddit and Facebook pages count even when their title does not say so; games from Steam, Epic, Roblox, Riot and others, and browsers and Steam games on Linux, are recognised too. Music (Spotify) is not slack.
- **Calmer switching**: a look at another customer's window now counts for the current customer for a minute instead of 10 seconds (*Ignore switches shorter than*). A value you set yourself stays.
