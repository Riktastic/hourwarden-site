- **Reports**: figures for total, billable share, time per day worked, customers and unknown time, each against the period before; time per day, week or month stacked by customer, with the target and details on hover; click a customer for its projects and a project for its tasks; a heatmap of when you work. Quarters, years and the last 12 months are new periods.
- **Your plan in the app**: Sync shows the plan and its use of customers, storage and devices; Reports says when a period reaches back further than the server keeps hours.
- **Mail through Brevo** (or any SMTP server), set up in the admin panel with a test mail.
- **Encrypted off-site backups**: each daily backup is also copied to S3-compatible storage, without stored secrets and encrypted with a backup key the administrator keeps.
- **Restore one account**: on an account's page the administrator puts back that account's data from a backup, after a backup of now; encrypted data stays encrypted.
