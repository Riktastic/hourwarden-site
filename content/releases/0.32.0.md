- **Quicker tasks**: `Ctrl+Alt+N` adds a task from anywhere and `Ctrl+N` in the app; on the Tasks screen `N`, `/`, arrows, `X`, `S` and `Esc`. Type due date and estimate as plain words (*Send report tomorrow 2h*, *volgende week*, *2u*), with a preview of what was understood, or set them with one click in the details or on the card itself.
- **A calmer Tasks screen**: no Pomodoro counts or hint texts, overdue and due-today tasks on top of To do, and *due tomorrow* instead of a date. The details put the checklist and notes first; project and ticket are under *More*. The details pane can be made wider or narrower, and notes take typed Markdown.
- **Undo**: ticking off or deleting a task shows *Undo* for a few seconds (also `Ctrl+Z`); deleting no longer asks first.
- **Customer lists with search** on Tasks, the Daily review and Reports.
- **A floating task list** that stays on top: pin it, fold it, hide it (`Ctrl+Alt+L`), or use the minimal view with only the next thing to tick off. Add tasks and steps from it.
- **Pomodoro** lives in the floating toolbar only.
- **Tasks on the phone**, with a home screen widget to tick them off; adding one understands the same words and has buttons for date and estimate.
- **Account pages**: Overview, Devices, Plan, Support and Security each have their own page; *Messages* is now *Support*. The Plan page lists the other plans to choose (Stripe) or request from the administrator.
- **Admin panel** in the same design, still on its own port.
- **Fixes**: the top bar no longer freezes the app when screens change (undocking a laptop); time asleep no longer counts as a freeze; SQLite 3.54 against RUSTSEC-2026-0459.
