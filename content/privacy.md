This page lists what HourWarden stores, where, and who can read it.

## On your computer

The app reads the window in front of you (its title, program, browser profile and folder) to decide which customer you are working for. **That raw activity does not leave your computer.** Only the result is stored: the customer, the times and the task you typed, in a database on your own device. You can delete it at any time.

## Sync (optional)

With an account on app.hourwarden.com your devices share customers, rules, tasks and hours.

- The connection is always encrypted (HTTPS).
- With **end-to-end encryption** turned on, your data is encrypted on your devices with a key only you have. The server stores it and has no key to read it, so we cannot read it either.
- The server keeps your account name, a hash of your password, your devices and when they last synced.
- For security, sign-ins, registrations and newly connected devices are logged with the internet address they came from. These logs are deleted after 30 days.
- The server is hosted in the European Union.

## No tracking

This website and the app use no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

## Your rights

You can remove devices and change your password on your account page. Ask us to remove your account and everything stored with it, and we remove it.
