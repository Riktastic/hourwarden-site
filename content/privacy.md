HourWarden is built so that your working day stays yours.

## On your computer

The app looks at the window in front (its title, program, browser profile, folder) to decide which customer you are working for. **That raw activity never leaves your computer.** Only the result — the customer, the times and the task you typed — is stored, in a database on your own device. You can delete it at any time.

## Sync (optional)

With an account on app.hourwarden.com your devices share customers, rules, tasks and hours.

- The connection is always encrypted (HTTPS).
- With **end-to-end encryption** turned on, your data is encrypted on your devices with a key only you have. The server stores it but cannot read it — neither can we.
- The server keeps your account name, a hash of your password, your devices and when they last synced.
- The server is hosted in the European Union.

## No tracking

This website and the app use no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

## Your rights

You can remove devices and change your password on your account page. Ask us to remove your account and everything stored with it; we do so without delay.
